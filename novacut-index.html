Privacy

Privacy Policy

NovaCut Pro is developed by CC DESIGN. This policy explains, in plain language, how the app treats your videos and your information.

Your videos stay yours No uploads, no account, no tracking. Editing happens entirely on your device.

Editing stays on your device

All video editing is processed locally on your device. Your videos and projects are never uploaded to our servers.

No account required

NovaCut works fully without an account, sign-in or registration.

No personal data collected or sold

We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Media permission, only for editing

NovaCut asks for access to your photos and videos only so you can pick media to edit and save your finished exports.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on your device. Clear the app storage in your device settings, or uninstall NovaCut, and it is all deleted.
